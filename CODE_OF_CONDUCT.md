# code of conduct

We want bloc to be a respectful, accessible place to collaborate, regardless of
experience, identity, background, disability, or viewpoint.

Be considerate, give constructive feedback, credit others, and focus criticism
on ideas and code. Respect privacy and boundaries. Harassment, discriminatory
remarks, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and persistent disruption are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, warn participants, restrict
participation, or ban participants according to severity and repetition. They
should explain decisions privately when practical and handle reports discreetly.

Report concerns privately to Rodrigo Dalla Dea (@rodalladea) at
[rodalladea@catap.io](mailto:rodalladea@catap.io). Do not disclose incident details
in public issues. A maintainer involved in a report must recuse themselves from
deciding it; where available, another uninvolved maintainer reviews the case and
any appeal. Rodrigo is currently the sole maintainer, so an independent project
review or appeal is not currently available.
